Publish as Android App (APK)

  1. Save File: Save this HTML file as index.html on your computer or phone.
  2. Host Online (Free): Upload index.html to Netlify Drop (app.netlify.com/drop) or GitHub Pages to get your live HTTPS link.
  3. Convert to Android APK: Visit WebIntoApp.com or Median.co, paste your live link, set App Name to ANIL BHOGTA, upload your 'A' icon, and download the .apk file.
  4. Install or Publish: Install the APK directly on Android phones, or upload the .aab bundle to Google Play Console.
